## LinkedIn (recommended)

I built **SQL Optima** as a personal project: an open-source, self-hosted monitoring and tuning assistant for **PostgreSQL** and **SQL Server**.

Not just charts — EXPLAIN analysis, best-practice rules, workload drilldowns, HA/RPO-RTO views, backup/DR readiness, and intelligence-style health reports. One Go binary + vanilla JS UI; `docker compose up` and you're exploring your estate in minutes.

It's still **beta** — my first primitive release — so you may hit rough edges. That's exactly why I'm sharing it: I'd love DBAs, SREs, and devs to **try it, break it, and tell me what to improve**.

Open source. Contributions and feedback welcome.

## Reddit / HN intro (one paragraph)

**SQL Optima** is the open-source SQL tuning assistant I wish I had as a consultant: live + historical dashboards for PostgreSQL and SQL Server, EXPLAIN bottlenecks, 15+ best-practice evaluators, backup/DR posture, HA replication with RPO/RTO, and printable intelligence reports — all self-hosted (Go + TimescaleDB, no SaaS telemetry). I'm releasing **v0.5.0** as an early beta from a solo builder; expect limitations, but `docker compose up` gets you a full UI in ~5 minutes.
